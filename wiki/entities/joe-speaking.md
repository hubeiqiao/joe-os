# Joe Speaking

Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Summary

Joe Speaking is Joe's current flagship product. It should be framed as an IELTS speaking simulator and English-speaking practice product built from Joe's own long-term communication pain, not as a detached market-first startup.

The product lineage matters. Joe Speaking grows out of the earlier IELTS Speaking Simulator GPT and represents a move from a useful public experiment into a more serious standalone product and company-building effort.

## Stable Framing

- flagship product, not the full roadmap
- built by a learner, for learners
- focused on real speaking, repetition, feedback, review, and confidence
- part of a broader founder trajectory under Just Joe Technologies Inc.
