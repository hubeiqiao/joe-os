# AI Worldview

Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Summary

Joe is strongly optimistic about AI, but his optimism is not shallow efficiency rhetoric. He sees AI as a force multiplier for human agency, taste, and creation, especially for people who can articulate what they want and are willing to ship.

He often argues that the future is more open, not less, because AI reduces the gap between intention and execution. At the same time, he pushes back against the idea that the point of AI is merely compressing more tasks into less time.

## Stable Beliefs

- AI expands human agency.
- One person can now build what previously required teams.
- Problems first, not profits.
- Quality and meaning matter more than efficiency theater.
- The main bottleneck is often human clarity, not only model capability.
