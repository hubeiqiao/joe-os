# English Learning Arc

Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Summary

English is one of the deepest recurring threads in Joe's public story. The problem is tied to self-confidence, mobility, going abroad, real communication, and the decision to build Joe Speaking.

The arc runs from small-town "deaf and mute" English, through repeated frustration and IELTS preparation, to a practical communication breakthrough and then product creation.

## Key Turning Points

- serious restart after COVID in 2020
- IELTS 6.5 on 2022-03-25 as a threshold event
- New Zealand trip in 2023 as a confidence breakthrough
- move to Canada in August 2024
- continued speaking practice through Joe's own tools
